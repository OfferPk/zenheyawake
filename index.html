Rules of Heyawake (ヘヤワケ)

Heyawake ("Divided Rooms") is an elegant Japanese logic puzzle invented by Nikoli. Partition tea rooms with black ink stones to bring harmony to the monastery!

1. Room Clue Numbers

Thick wooden lattice lines divide the floor into rectangular rooms. A number in the top-left of a room indicates exactly how many black stones belong in that room. Rooms without numbers can contain any count.

2. No Adjacent Black Stones

Black stones (●) can never touch orthogonally (sharing an edge). Diagonal touching is allowed.

3. Single Connected White Complex

All white floor cells must form a single connected group. No white cell may be walled off or isolated.

4. The Corridor Constraint

A straight horizontal or vertical line of white cells cannot cross through more than two rooms. Any uninterrupted straight corridor of white cells may visit at most 2 distinct rooms before being blocked by an ink stone or the grid border.

💡 Controls & Tips

  • Tap a cell to cycle: Empty → Black Stone (●) → White Dot (·) → Empty.
  • Or choose Stone or Dot tool mode to drag and paint swiftly across cells.
  • Room clue numbers turn Green when properly satisfied!
  • A 2×2 room with clue 2 must have diagonal black stones.
  • Use dots (·) to confirm cells that cannot be black.
