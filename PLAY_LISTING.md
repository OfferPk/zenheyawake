## Full Description
Step into a tranquil Japanese monastery garden. ZenHeyawake brings the revered Nikoli pencil-and-paper logic puzzle **Heyawake** (ヘヤワケ, "Divided Rooms") to your mobile device with handcrafted elegance, peaceful aesthetics, and procedural tea room acoustics.

### The Art of Room Division
Guide dark ink stones across tatami floor tiles and shoji screen lattice divisions:
1. **Room Numbers**: Each room indicates the exact number of black stones hidden within its borders. Unnumbered rooms can hold any count.
2. **No Adjacent Stones**: Black stones must never touch horizontally or vertically. Diagonal touching is allowed.
3. **Harmonious White Complex**: Every white floor cell must remain interconnected in a single continuous path.
4. **The Corridor Rule**: An uninterrupted straight line of white cells cannot cross through more than two rooms. Break long corridors with thoughtfully placed ink stones!

### Key Features
- **50 Handcrafted Levels**: 5 difficulty tiers ranging from beginner Engawa (6x6) to master Daitokuji (10x10).
- **100% Unique Solutions**: Every puzzle is verified by deductive logic constraints. No guesswork required.
- **Procedural Acoustic Engine**: Authentic wooden shoji clicks, pebble stone taps, bamboo ticks, harmonic koto chords, and resonant Bonshō temple bells generated live through pure audio synthesis.
- **Intuitive Touch Controls**: Seamlessly tap or drag to paint black stones or mark white dots.
- **Smart Deductive Hint Engine**: Receive gentle, logical guidance whenever you need insight into forced placements or room restrictions.
- **Unlimited Undo & Reset**: Solve at your own serene pace without time pressure.
- **Completely Offline & Private**: Zero advertisements, zero user tracking, zero data collection. Plays flawlessly without an internet connection.
